Privacy Policy

Invoice Generator is designed to protect your privacy. The tools on this site run entirely in your web browser. Anything you type into a tool is processed on your own device and is never sent to a server, stored by this site, or shared with anyone.

This site does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect names, email addresses or payment details. The contact page lists an email address, and if you choose to email us, your message is used only to reply to you.

Some tools save your own data in your own browser, for example a task list, using your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser data. This site does not see it.

This site may show third-party advertising in the future. Any advertising provider would use its own cookies under its own policy. This privacy policy may be updated from time to time, and the latest version is always shown on this page.
